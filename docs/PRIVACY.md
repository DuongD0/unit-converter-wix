# Unit Converter privacy policy

Unit Converter does not collect, store or send any personal information.

The app keeps a list of your last 50 conversions so you can see them again after restarting it.
That list is saved only on your own computer, in
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\UnitConverter\history.json`. It never leaves the device, and you can delete it
at any time with the **Clear history** button or by deleting the file.

The app makes no network connections, uses no analytics and shows no advertising.

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue in this repository.
